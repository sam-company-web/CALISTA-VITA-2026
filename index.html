Calista Vita is a lifestyle brand created for women who love individuality, natural beauty and effortless bohemian style.

We carefully curate jewellery, handbags, boho-inspired clothing, perfume, handmade lifestyle pieces and naturally scented candles and home fragrance, bringing together fashion and everyday rituals that add personality, warmth and elegance to life.
